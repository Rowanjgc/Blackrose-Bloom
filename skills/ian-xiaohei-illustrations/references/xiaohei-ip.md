# Xiaohei IP

## Character Definition

Xiaohei is the fixed visual IP for Ian-style English article illustrations.

Every image should include Xiaohei. Xiaohei is not a mascot, a sticker, or a cute decoration. Xiaohei is a serious absurd worker who participates in the system.

## Appearance

- One continuous upright bean/capsule-shaped SOLID BLACK ink body, about 1.6
  times as tall as wide, with a slightly irregular hand-drawn outline.
- The upper body is also the head. No separate round head, neck, shoulders,
  human torso, or outline-only body.
- Exactly two equal small WHITE DOT eyes in the upper quarter, horizontally
  aligned. Each eye is about one tenth of the body width, spaced about two eye
  diameters apart. No mouth, nose, eyebrows, highlights, or facial outline.
- Two thin black stick arms and two thin black stick legs, each roughly half
  the body height, ending bluntly without detailed fingers or shoes.
- Blank deadpan expression. No fur, cat ears, tail, clothing, or accessories.

Keep these proportions and features across images. The plan controls pose,
facing, position, size within the frame, and physical contact. It does not
reinvent the body shape. Use a front or three-quarter view with both eyes
visible. Keep the creature clearly readable, physically engaged with the
main object. The name alone does not specify this design to the image model.

## Personality

- Serious, but the thing being done is a little absurd.
- Like a low-key system operator.
- Dry humor, not cute.
- A little clumsy, but not dumb.
- Feels like a real worker in a whiteboard sketch.

## Common Roles

Let Xiaohei perform the core action:

- Carry materials.
- Pull lines to gather information sources.
- Get stuck at a breakpoint.
- Operate a "decision" lever inside a machine.
- Hold or operate a filtering funnel while retaining the fixed bean body.
- Cut open a "material fish".
- Stamp a handoff phrase.
- Pull a handoff path.
- Hold up a warning sign to point out pitfalls.
- Reach out from a hole but fail to catch the content.
- Carry bricks, build bridges, open doors, sort, and record.

## Don'ts

- Do not make Xiaohei overly cute.
- Do not draw Xiaohei as a children's cartoon character.
- Do not give Xiaohei complex clothing, sticker faces, or shiny eyes.
- Do not let Xiaohei stand at the edge doing nothing.
- Do not let Xiaohei steal the structure explanation.
- Do not make Xiaohei too commercial, too rounded, or too polished.

## Rule of Thumb

If removing Xiaohei still leaves the core metaphor intact, Xiaohei is too decorative. Rewrite the prompt so Xiaohei becomes the action subject.
